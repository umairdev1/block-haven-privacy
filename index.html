Privacy Policy

Block Haven · Last updated: 25 November 2026

Block Haven ("the game") is a relaxing block puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information the game handles.

Information we collect

The game does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (scores, best score, daily challenge streak, statistics, achievements and your in-progress game) and settings (sound, music, vibration) are saved only on your own device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes them. If you use Android's device backup, Android may include this data in your personal Google backup.

Internet and permissions

The game works fully offline and does not request internet access. The only permission it uses is vibration, for optional haptic feedback.

Advertising and purchases

This version contains no advertising and no in-app purchases. If a future version adds them, this policy will be updated before that version is released, explaining what data those services use.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
